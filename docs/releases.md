# Releases

<!-- This page belongs to the project, and docs/releases.de.md is its German translation: change both together. The hooks put the releases of every project in place of the marker below while MkDocs builds the website, with their notes from GitHub. -->

What changed in every project, release by release: the notes of the latest five releases of each, the newest first, in the words of their users, as each project writes them into its changelog. A release that came out after this page was built shows up below the heading of its project, with a link to its notes on GitHub. The [feed of all releases](https://dennis-otto.github.io/repo-blueprint/dashboard/releases.atom) brings each new one into a feed reader, and the [dashboard](https://dennis-otto.github.io/repo-blueprint/dashboard/) shows the state of every project.
