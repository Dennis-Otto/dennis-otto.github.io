### Features

- **The numbers of every project on its card, and what's new:** the home page said in one sentence that the projects come with tests and checks, without a number. Every card of a project now shows its latest release and how long ago it came out, the share of its code that its tests run, its mutation score, its OpenSSF Scorecard, the median time to the first answer by a person to a new issue when there is one, and the level of its OpenSSF Best Practices, such as *v1.17.0 · released today · 100% test coverage · 90% mutation score · Scorecard 7.8 · OpenSSF Best Practices: silver*, in German on the German home page. *What's new* below the projects lists the newest release of each project, each linked to its notes. The numbers come from the dashboard at the same address and follow it within two hours; the page asks no other server. Without JavaScript, a card shows the level of its Best Practices and *What's new* links the dashboard. The head of both home pages names the feed of the releases of all projects, so a feed reader finds it from the address of the website.
